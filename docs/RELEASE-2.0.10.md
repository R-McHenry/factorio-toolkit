# Toolkit 2.0.10: what to do after a big step

Rory, 2026-10-23, after 2.0.9's rule on waiting: "go to activities after doing a big step should be
looking at that step, planning other things, looking for things that can be cleaned up or improved,
checking that the big step is operational".

- The waiting section of core-v1 and lead-v1 names the go-to after a big step, such as a build
  pasted or a block rebuilt or standing: look at it, check that it's operational, look for what can be
  cleaned up or improved, and plan the next things. While something runs, do the same for another
  part of the base.
- The worker brief: after a big step, look at it, check that it works, and note what could be
  cleaned up or improved for the report.
- The Stop hook under test says it in every nudge.
