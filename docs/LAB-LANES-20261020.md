# Lane and inserter lab, 2026-10-20

`python tools/lab_lanes.py` on a laptop test server: the laptop's Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age,
quality and elevated rails active, never the live game. Every fixture stands on the lab surface,
on a force of its own with no research (`v2lab`). The raw numbers go to `out/lab-lanes.json`
(not committed). L is transport line 1 and R is line 2, looking along the flow.

## The lane rules: every one holds as Rory gave it

| case | want | got |
|---|---|---|
| inserter across the belt, on its left (a belt flowing east, and one flowing north) | R | R |
| inserter across the belt, on its right | L | L |
| inserter in line, behind the belt (the belt flows away from it) | R | R |
| inserter in line, ahead of the belt (the belt flows toward it) | R | R |
| a belt side-loads from the target's left (a belt behind the target) | both lanes onto L | both onto L |
| a belt side-loads from the target's right | both lanes onto R | both onto R |
| a belt fed from one side with nothing behind it (a turn) | lanes kept | lanes kept |
| side-load onto an underground entrance from its left, with or without a belt behind it | the open-side lane (copper) onto L, the other (iron) blocked | the same |
| side-load onto an underground exit from its left | the open-side lane (iron) onto L, copper blocked | the same |
| side-load onto an underground entrance from its right | iron onto R, copper blocked | the same |
| a splitter, iron on L and copper on R coming in | lanes kept on both outputs | lanes kept, 10 and 10 out of each side |
| an electric drill facing south, and one facing north, onto a belt flowing east | its near lane: L, and R | L, and R |
| a burner drill facing south | L | L |

Recorded where no rule was given yet:

| case | got |
|---|---|
| inserter onto a turn, from the turn's left | R (the far lane) |
| inserter onto an underground entrance, across from its left | R, and on into the tunnel |
| inserter onto an entrance in line, from behind (its open half) or from ahead (its hood) | R both ways, into the tunnel |
| inserter onto an underground exit: across from its left, in line from behind (its hood), in line from ahead | R every way: **an exit takes drops** |
| inserter onto a splitter's left half, from the left | R, out of both outputs |

**A V1 finding.** `fkcore.connect` holds that an underground exit takes no drop (v4's B-120), and
the router never taps one. In 2.0.77 an exit takes a drop from every side. The router's caution
costs nothing, but the V1 judge can call a net OPEN where an inserter does feed it. V2's lane-aware
judge has the measured rule.

## Picking from a belt with two items

One fast inserter, a stopped belt, one item on each lane:

| into | got |
|---|---|
| a stone furnace, from iron-ore on L and coal on R | ore and coal both: 12 plates made, 5 coal and 2 ore inside |
| an assembler on gears, from iron-plate on L and copper-plate on R | iron only: 5 gears made, no copper inside |
| a steel chest, from iron-plate on L and copper-plate on R | both, 16 and 16 |

An inserter takes from both lanes whatever its target accepts. Into a chest it merges the two
nets; into a machine it takes only what the recipe or the fuel slot uses.

Every inserter has 5 filter slots in 2.0.77, the burner too. Belt speeds 0.03125, 0.0625, 0.09375
and 0.125 tiles per tick (yellow to turbo, as the catalog has them); underground reach 5, 7, 9 and

force (everything researched) has inserter +3, bulk +11 and belt stack +3.

## Inserter rates

Items/s over 30 game seconds each. The hand columns are the force's bonuses: inserter 0, 1, 2
(every non-bulk hand 1, 2, 3) and bulk 0, 1, 3 (a bulk hand 1, 2, 4; a stack inserter's 5, 6, 8).

| inserter | hand | chest to chest | belt to chest (a full belt, moving) | chest to belt (an empty belt, moving) | 60 x rotation x hand |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| burner-inserter | 1 / 2 / 3 | 0.80 / 1.60 / 2.40 | | | 0.78 / 1.56 / 2.34 |
| inserter | 1 / 2 / 3 | 0.87 / 1.73 / 2.60 | 1.00 / 1.80 / 2.50 | 0.83 / 1.67 / 2.30 | 0.84 / 1.68 / 2.52 |
| long-handed-inserter | 1 / 2 / 3 | 1.20 / 2.40 / 3.60 | | | 1.2 / 2.4 / 3.6 |
| fast-inserter | 1 / 2 / 3 | 2.50 / 5.06 / 7.50 | 2.50 / 4.99 / 7.00 | 2.50 / 4.80 / 5.63 | 2.4 / 4.8 / 7.2 |
| bulk-inserter | 1 / 2 / 4 | 2.50 / 4.99 / 9.99 | 2.50 / 4.99 / 8.66 | 2.50 / 4.80 / 6.00 | 2.4 / 4.8 / 9.6 |
| stack-inserter | 5 / 6 / 8 | 12.5 / 15.2 / 20.0 | | | 12.0 / 14.4 / 19.2 |

A full yellow belt between two loaders carries 14.99/s, 7.5 a lane.

What it says:

- The rule of thumb holds chest to chest, within 4%. The fast and bulk inserters make 2.5 a hand,
  not 2.4: a swing of 12 ticks rather than 12.5.
- From a full moving belt it holds up to hand 2, then loses a little: fast at hand 3 makes 93% of
  its chest rate, bulk at hand 4 makes 87%.
- Onto a moving belt it holds at hands 1 and 2, then falls off: fast at hand 3 makes 75%, bulk at
  hand 4 makes 60% (a yellow belt; the hand goes down onto the belt one item at a time).
