## New findings (reported, not detoured on)

1. **The v4 rate rule has a degenerate fixed point at every supply = demand**, adapters aside: a
   consumer cut by short supply and a producer cut by low demand each keep the other low. My first
   port reproduced it (red stuck at 0.53, gears at 0.34, each "binding" on the other). V1 judges
   each side of a net against the other side's potential, so it can't happen. Also fixed:
   proportional sharing that scaled by potential oscillated across nets, so shares are now
   water-filled in units of duty (a short net runs everyone it limits at the same fraction of their
   duty-capped appetite).
2. **spm10 never balanced iron** (11.8/s asked of 5/s, above). v4 couldn't see it.
3. **The mall takes 31% of the adv circuits even with unlimited plates.** At duty 0.02 its
   roboport cell asks 0.09 adv/s (45 per craft). The duty stand-in is crude; the V1 boundary rate
   (`Chest<provider, rate=...>`) is the better knob once the mall's real restock rate is known.
4. **Three-ingredient rows** (inserter, engine, blue): first refused with a note rather than a
   guessed layout; now built as you chose, a third belt south of the product belt reached by
   long-handed inserters. The same geometry tests cover it in all four rotations.
5. **Alerts are per player in the API** (`LuaPlayer.get_alerts`), and agent runs have no
   character, so the alert push needs a check on the host (now open in CORE-V1 §13).
6. **The lab shares the real game's UPS** (one server), so full-rate measuring in the lab is not
   free while the container is CPU-capped.
7. Naming: `v5` (the repo) is not `v4-hand\live\v5` (the old spm10 base folder).
